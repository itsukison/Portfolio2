APPROACH
(Approach)
Most AI products are built for people who already know how to use AI. I build for the ones who don't.
The capability exists. What's missing is the interface that makes it feel like it was always meant for you. That gap is almost never technical — it's a design problem, and it's the most important one of the decade.

ABOUT ME
(Design + Engineering)
I'm Itsuki Son, a design engineer based in Tokyo. I let layout, proportion, and negative space do the guiding — so a first-time user moves through a product like someone moving through a well-designed room. Unhurried. Like they've been here before.
I'm drawn to work where technology changes someone's actual circumstances — projects like bitchat, which gives political voice to the suppressed, or tools that do what decades of microfinance policy couldn't. If you're thinking about a startup at that intersection, I'd love to talk.

PHILOSOPHY
(new section)
(Philosophy)

"I have led a toothless life, he thought. A toothless life. I have never bitten into anything. I was waiting. I was reserving myself for later on — and I have just noticed that my teeth have gone."
— Jean-Paul Sartre

I think about this passage more than I'd like to admit.
There is a version of a life that is spent in careful preparation. Accumulating the right credentials, waiting for the right moment, building toward something that never quite arrives because the waiting becomes the habit.
I don't want that life. I want to be someone who bites. Who takes on problems that are too large, ships things before they're ready, and treats the present moment as the only one that's actually available.
Becoming a better person — kinder, more capable, more honest — is not a background goal for me. It's the whole point. The products are how I practice.
